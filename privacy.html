Privacy & Trust

Your inquiry stays focused on your request.

This page explains what happens when you submit a Free Review request, which services help process it, and how to contact me about your information.

Information you choose to share

The form asks for your name, email, business name, and a description of your business and automation needs. Website, tools, and phone are optional. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

How I use it

I use your details to review your request, contact you about the Free Review, and follow up on work you ask about. I do not sell Free Review submissions or use them to make automated decisions about you.

Where the request goes

The form passes through Cloudflare Pages to my n8n workflow. The workflow records the inquiry in Google Sheets and sends notification and confirmation emails through Gmail. These providers process the information needed to perform those steps.

Form protection

The form uses input checks and rate limiting to reduce automated abuse. Cloudflare Turnstile may check whether a submission is legitimate. These measures help protect the form but cannot guarantee that every unwanted request is blocked.

Access and retention

I keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and manage the resulting conversation. Access to the Sheet and email account is limited to the accounts used for this workflow. If you would like your inquiry corrected or removed, contact me using the address below.

Contact

For questions about a Free Review inquiry or your information, email hello@anyalazarenko.com. Please include enough detail to identify your request, but do not send sensitive credentials.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What happens in the free review?

You tell me what's eating your time through the form below. I look at your actual process and tell you what's realistically automatable, what should stay human, and what a practical first version could look like. No commitment, no sales pitch.

How much will it cost?

It depends on scope, which is exactly what the free review figures out. After it, you get a plain-English proposal with scope and price — you decide from there, before committing to anything.

How long does it take?

I start with the smallest automation that's actually useful, so you see value fast — then we expand from there. You'll know the timeline for your specific case in the proposal.

Do I need to change the tools I already use?

No. I connect the tools you already have — n8n, Make, APIs, AI agents, and the rest of your stack. New tools only enter the picture when there's a clear reason.

Will I be able to run it myself afterwards?

Yes. Every engagement ends with a handoff: a working system plus a clear explanation of how it runs. No black boxes, no dependency on me for day-to-day operation.

What if my work shouldn't be automated?

Then I'll tell you honestly. Some things should stay human, and identifying those is part of the review — I'd rather tell you “don't automate this” than sell you something you don't need.

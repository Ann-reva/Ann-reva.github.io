SET UP ONCE

One-time prompts (3, 4, 5): paste into a new chat. Done.

Daily prompts (1, 2): do not hunt for a menu. Paste the prompt in a chat and tell the assistant to run it every morning and send you the result. ChatGPT and Gemini turn that into a scheduled task. Claude: scheduled tasks in the desktop app.

No schedule option in your app? Run the prompt yourself each morning. Still faster than checking everything by hand.

1. THE ACTION-ONLY INBOX

Read my new emails in [Gmail]. Show me only the ones that need an action from me. For each one: who sent it, what they need, and the deadline. Skip newsletters, receipts and updates. If nothing needs me, say exactly that: nothing needs you today.
SAMPLE OUTPUT — WHAT YOU GET BACK
• Client — the signed contract is back, needs your signature — today
• Landlord — lease renewal attached — by Friday
• 14 newsletters, receipts and updates — skipped

You need: an assistant connected to your inbox — Claude or ChatGPT with a Gmail connector, or Gemini with Google Workspace (paid plan).

2. THE STATUS WATCHER

Check [paste the link — a shipping page, a public status page, a job board] every morning at [8:00]. Tell me only if the status changed since yesterday, and say what changed. If nothing changed, one line is enough: no change.

You need: one link you currently check by hand — a page that opens without a login. Scheduled tasks cannot see pages behind your password.

3. THE PRE-FLIGHT CHECK

Here is my draft: [paste it]. Before I publish, check it against [the event page / the brief / the source numbers] and list every mismatch you find. Do not rewrite it. Only list what does not match.

You need: nothing but the draft and the source it has to match.

4. THE BOOKING LINK

The scheduling rule that ends the back-and-forth: never negotiate times in messages. One link does it.

Here is my booking link: [paste your Calendly or Cal.com link]. Draft a two-sentence reply to [paste their message] that shares the link and sounds like me: warm, short, no sales talk.

You need: a free booking page (Calendly or Cal.com). Five minutes to create, once.

5. THE LIVE-CHECK RULE

This one is not a chat prompt. Paste it into your assistant's custom instructions or project settings, once.

Before you tell me something is not done, outdated or unavailable, check the live source first — [the page / the file / the calendar] — and show me what you found, with the date you checked it. Never report 'not done' from memory.

You need: an assistant with custom instructions (ChatGPT: Settings, then Personalization. Claude: a Project).

BEFORE YOU CONNECT ANYTHING

Privacy, honestly: automation 1 works only if an AI can read your inbox. That is real access to private mail. Connect only an assistant you trust — and start with one automation, not all five.
